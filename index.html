Events & Messages

Internal Note
For your own records — the vendor cannot see this. Use for notes like "EU might want to switch vendors."

Select "Notify Workflow" if you need action from credit or workflow, e.g. "Please update the business name to XYZ."
Message to Vendor
Important: If the dropdown is set to "Complete," the vendor does not receive a message. Use "Complete" for your own general notes only.

If you want the vendor to be notified, check "Send email on create?" to send it directly to them.
"Hey JP! We have this customer approved. I spoke to them — they'll confirm the order with you. Please submit the invoice when ready so I can go over terms and work on funding."
Vendor notifications
Go to Vendor Account → Contacts → Application Communication Preferences and confirm the "New Requirements" box is checked. Without this, your Events & Messages notes won't reach the vendor.
